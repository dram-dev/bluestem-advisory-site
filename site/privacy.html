Privacy

Effective October 22, 2026

Bluestem Advisory LLC is an Illinois consulting firm. This page explains what we collect through this website and through the private tools that connect to our own Google accounts, and what we do with it.

On this website

What we collect. Only what you choose to send us:

  • Ask a question: your name, email, organization and its type, and your question.
  • The assessment: your answers, plus your name, email and organization, so we can send you your write-up.

With either form we also note the page you sent it from, the site that sent you to us, and any campaign tags in the link. We don't keep your IP address, only a one-way code made from it, which we use to stop spam.

No tracking. The site uses no analytics or advertising cookies, and no tracking scripts.

Who handles it. Netlify hosts the site and Cloudflare protects it. Its fonts load from Google Fonts, so your browser fetches them from Google. What you send passes through Netlify to our own client system. That system runs on our own computer, with an encrypted database and encrypted backups. Your assessment write-up is emailed to you through our email provider, Resend.

How we use it. To answer you, send what you asked for, and follow up about working together. We never sell or rent your information. We share it only with the providers above, to run the site, or when the law requires.

How long we keep it. As long as it helps us serve you or keep proper business records. Ask us, and we'll delete it.

Our tools that use Google accounts

macro-ai-digest is our private tool. It reads one label of newsletters in our own Gmail account to build a daily briefing for us. It has read-only access, so it can't send, change or delete mail. It processes the messages with AI that runs on our own computer and keeps the results in our own private storage. The briefing goes only to our own phones, through a private Telegram bot. We don't share, sell or transfer this data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, email admin@bluestemadvisoryllc.com. If we change this policy, we'll update this page and its date.
