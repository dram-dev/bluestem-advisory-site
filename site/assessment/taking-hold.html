Still underground

The direction is in the soil more than above it — and that's true whether it's a strategic plan nobody opens or a shared understanding nobody has written down. Most of the organizations we're proudest of working with started here. It isn't a strategy problem so much as a ground problem: the why hasn't been said out loud together, so nothing beneath it connects.
