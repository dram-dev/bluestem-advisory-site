Unexamined

The plan may well be right, but the ground under it hasn't been looked at: what it rests on is treated as known, the people it affects were mostly the people who wrote it, nothing was ruled out, and the person holding a key process together has no name on paper. None of that shows until something moves — a leader leaves, a member leaves, an assumption turns out wrong.
