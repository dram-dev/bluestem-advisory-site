About us

Lyndsey Girod Ramsey

Founder & Principal

Lyndsey's career began in the soil — studying its characteristics and writing conservation plans alongside farmers, where the lesson took root early: what lies beneath the surface decides what stands above it.

Her work started in science, data, mapping, and careful reading of a landscape. Ever since, Lyndsey has carried her discipline outward, putting it to work for a far greater circle: farmers, organizations, and the communities they hold together. Along her journey, Lyndsey learned to listen at scale to thousands of members, volunteers, and board members, to turn what people say into direction they're proud to own.

Bluestem Advisory carries that arc forward: understand how an organization truly works before trying to move it, and help it close the gap between where it is and where it says it wants to be. Patient work, deep roots, plans that stand strong.

Where it comes from
Frame

Before anything is drafted, the conversation gets set up to produce something worth acting on: the questions the organization actually needs to answer, a map of who should be heard, a calendar for the whole process, and sessions designed to evoke real discussion.

Listen

Then comes evidence — from inside, outside, and ahead: survey results you keep and can re-run, themes and verbatim quotes from interviews, a clear look at how peers position and where the open ground sits, and the trends the plan has to hold up against.

Decide

Wide input becomes choices leadership will stand behind: facilitated sessions where nothing said in the room is lost, a synthesis of where alignment already exists, the directions the organization commits to — with written reasons for the ones declined — and priorities ranked against criteria agreed to in advance.

Advance

The plan made real inside your organization: roles and a meeting cadence that keep it alive, messages tuned to each audience, a workplan where every initiative can be monitored, and a scoring rhythm that shows where progress is made.
